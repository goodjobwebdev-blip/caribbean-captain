# Repeatable quest roadmap

Agreed direction: 24 September 2026. These are repeatable job types, not one-off story campaigns. Difficulty describes the simplest satisfying version against the current engine. All five easy types are included in this release; medium and hard work remains proposed.

## Easy — reuse delivery, cargo, and passenger mechanics

| Building | Quest | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Church | Carry monks | Passenger contracts between churches. |
| Church | Donation requests | Small local silver requests, with standing rewards and repeat limits. Goods donations can be added later. |
| Pharmacy | Medical deliveries | Sealed herbs, remedies, and medical supplies between pharmacies; not usable medicines. |
| Fort & Garrison | Ammunition and gunpowder resupply | Sealed freight between garrisons. |
| Governor | Very important letters | Official dispatches with different availability and rewards; no interception or secrecy mechanics. |

Implementation and provisional balance: [easy commissions](easy-commissions.md).

## Medium — new objectives or targeted encounters

| Building | Quest | New work required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shipyard | Collect repair materials | Track requested goods, consume them on return, and balance payment against procurement costs. |
| Fort & Garrison | Hunt wanted captains | Identify and spawn targets, track qualifying victories. Defeat/sinking is simpler than taking a captain alive. |
| Governor | High-value bounties | Bounty system with stronger targets and different rewards. |
| Store | Sink commercial competitors | Targeted merchant combat and explicit consequences. |
| Fort & Garrison / Governor | Pirate patrols | Patrol routes/areas, qualifying encounters, and completion conditions. |
| Tavern / Brothel | Wealthy merchant information | Reveal a particular merchant encounter for interception or plunder. |
| Brothel | Bounty-hunter leads | Reveal a wanted target using the bounty system. |
| Customs House | Deliver confiscated cargo | Special cargo status; easy if reduced to ordinary sealed official freight. |

Delivery deadlines are a medium shared feature: expiry, failure consequences, and Journal feedback. They are not part of the easy release.

## Hard — substantial new mechanics

| Building | Quest | Missing systems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tavern / Brothel | Treasure maps | Bays, coves, uninhabited islands and other exploration locations; clues, searching, and discovery. |
| Tavern / Harbour Master | Small / medium / large escorts | Friendly second ship, convoy travel, protection objectives, and multi-ship combat. Small escorts still need this foundation. |
| Shipyard | Escort an empty ship | Escort system plus a vulnerable vessel with a small crew. |
| Harbour Master | Find and capture stolen ships | Targets, capture conditions, and holding/returning a quest vessel with single-ship ownership. |
| Shipyard | Buy a ship elsewhere and return it | Second vessel, crew, ownership, and handover. |
| Tavern / Brothel | Ransom targets | Person tracking, prisoners, custody, and ransom delivery/negotiation. |
| Governor | Espionage in hostile ports | Infiltration, suspicion, investigation choices, and discovery consequences. |
| Governor | Capture ships with specified loot | Targeted cargo, capture objectives, preservation, and handover rules. |
| Bank | Seize money or ships from debtors | Debtor encounters, demands/refusal, seizure, and possibly returning a second vessel. |
| Customs House | Inspect suspicious ships | Player-led inspections, evidence, compliance/refusal, and enforcement. |
| Customs House | Anti-smuggling patrols | Patrols plus identifying contraband, inspections, and seizures. |
| Customs House | Recover confiscated cargo | Recovery encounters, identifiable cargo, and delivery tracking; could be medium with a simple combat-and-retrieve scope. |

## Already available before this release

- Store: small and medium freight.
- Harbour Master: letters, passengers, and large freight; legacy contracts still finish here.
- No quests planned for Harbour, Blacksmith, Book Store, or Weaver.

Quest rewards, exact objectives, and failure rules for deferred types still need discussion before implementation. Do not infer authorization for those features from this roadmap.
