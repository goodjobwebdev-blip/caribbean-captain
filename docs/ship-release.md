# Ship ownership release

## Included

- One owned ship instance with a stable identity, name, configuration, hull points,
  sail condition, and operational cannons in four batteries.
- 28 predefined configurations, six tiers, and Merchant / Universal / Warship classes.
- New captains inherit the Universal Sloop named The Wayfarer with the existing
  800 silver, 10 crew, and 120 provisions. Ship price is not deducted at creation.
- All configurations are available in all three shipyards. Filter by tier/class,
  compare specifications, review the exchange, and explicitly confirm.
- Full purchase price includes full repairs and the default cannon loadout.
  Sale value is max(0, floor(70% of purchase price minus quoted hull, sail,
  and missing-default-cannon repairs)). Cannons stay with the ship sold.
- The exchange applies the sale and purchase together with no game-time cost.
  Cheaper replacements can return silver. No purchase of the already-owned
  configuration. No purchase while failed or resolving a voyage encounter.
- Cargo, provisions, crew, passengers, contracts, skills, and history transfer.
  Purchase is blocked if there is insufficient silver, too much cargo, too many
  crew/passengers, insufficient deadweight, or fewer crew than the new ship requires. Nothing is silently
  discarded. Unaffordable ships can still be compared.
- Tavern release action: one sailor, no fee, one hour. Cannot release below the
  current ship's minimum. Wages and provisions still accrue. This supports
  downsizing; larger ships may require recruiting on an intermediate ship first.
- Voyage forecasts use the owned configuration's base speed, carried weight, hull, sails, crew, and the existing 5%-per-Sailing-tier multiplier. Current voyages retain their stored duration. See [performance formulas](ship-performance.md).
- Store, recruitment, contract acceptance, and failure checks use owned ship limits.
- Hull repair, sail repair, and default cannon replacement are separate services.
  See balance.md for costs and durations. Starvation can still interrupt services.
- Journal remains informational. It includes base/current ship statistics and
  identifies design ratings whose gameplay effects are deferred.

## Compatibility

Version 1 saves/checkpoints without a ship become version 2 states on load/action.
The legacy 0–100 condition maps to the starting sloop's 0–100 hull points.
Sails and default cannons begin intact. Existing damage, failure, treasury,
calendar, skills, random seed, commissions, and pending voyage duration persist.
Reading or restoring an old checkpoint does not overwrite that checkpoint.
New checkpoints preserve the purchased ship and all its condition fields.

## Scope limits

Goods prices, wages, provision costs, existing contract rewards, and Sailing point rewards have not been rebalanced. Larger 200/800-unit freight jobs extend the existing quantity-based pricing; see ship-performance.md. Prices for ships are the provisional revised
anchors plus variant-specific prices from ships.ts. Larger ship affordability
and operating economics need further playtesting.

Prototype encounter damage remains a percentage of maximum hull. Cannons,
protection, maneuverability, ship class, and tier do not modify dice outcomes.
Only hull damage is currently generated by encounters; separate sail/cannon
state and repair services are ready for later damage mechanics.

Deadweight and crew/condition-based performance are active. Shipyard comparisons
include the transferred load and current crew. Maneuverability is calculated for
reference; protection and cannon effects on combat remain deferred.

Capture, custom cannon outfitting, configuration conversion, multiple ships,
and ship-dependent Sailing training limits remain deferred.
