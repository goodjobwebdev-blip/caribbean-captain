# Town buildings

This document defines the intended commercial, service, and quest identity of town locations. The three current towns—Bridgetown, Saint-Pierre, and Willemstad—share the same location list for now.

Anything marked **TBD** is design information only. It is not an implemented action, reward, inventory system, or quest.

## Existing locations

### Harbour

The harbour remains the place for voyage preparation and departure rather than a literal building.

- **Buy and sell:** None.
- **Services — current:** Plan voyages, review routes and costs, prepare cannon battery loadouts, and depart for another port.
- **Quests:** None.

### Store

The Store is the legal commercial market and the source of ordinary freight work.

- **Buy and sell — current:** Legal market goods. Personal weapons have moved to the Blacksmith.
- **Buy and sell — TBD:** Remove smuggled goods from the Store. Contraband will be available only through a Tavern smuggler contact.
- **Services — TBD:** Store unwanted goods locally for a recurring fee. Stored goods remain in that town.
- **Quests — TBD:** Small and medium freight commissions moved from the Harbour Master; rare contracts to sink commercial competitors.

### Tavern

The Tavern remains the social and crew-services centre. It also becomes the gateway to rumours and illegal trade.

- **Buy and sell — TBD:** Buy and sell alcohol as inventory items; obtain smuggled goods exclusively through an arranged contact.
- **Services — current:** Hire or release sailors, sleep, train crew sailing/gunnery/fighting, receive medical care, take shore leave, and arrange a smuggler contact.
- **Quests — TBD:** Buy treasure maps; buy information about valuable ransom targets or wealthy merchants; accept small merchant escort work.

### Harbour Master

The Harbour Master provides official civilian commissions and permits.

- **Buy and sell — current:** National trade permits and a spyglass.
- **Services — current:** Accept commissions, deliver completed work, and collect rewards.
- **Quests — current:** Letters, freight, and passenger contracts.
- **Quests — TBD target:** Keep letters and passengers; move small and medium freight to Stores; retain large freight; add medium and large escorts; find and capture stolen ships.

### Shipyard

The Shipyard handles ships, repairs, armament, and ship-improvement work.

- **Buy and sell — current:** Buy ships while selling the current ship as part of the exchange.
- **Buy and sell — TBD:** Buy cannons separately. Replacing missing default cannons remains distinct from buying new or upgraded armament.
- **Services — current:** Repair hull and sails, replace missing cannons, use carried repair materials, compare ships, and exchange ships.
- **Services — TBD:** Rename and repaint ships; install upgrades such as cotton or silk sails, reinforced hulls, and other equipment.
- **Quests — TBD:** Buy a ship in another port and bring it back; collect repair materials from another port; escort an empty ship with a small crew.

### Church

The Church records recovery checkpoints and later offers religious or diplomatic work.

- **Buy and sell:** None.
- **Services — current:** Create, restore, and delete checkpoints.
- **Services — TBD:** Improve attitude with nations.
- **Quests — TBD:** Carry monks as passengers; complete small donation requests.

## Planned buildings

The following buildings are present in the town interface. Blacksmith and Weaver are now actionable; the other listed buildings remain non-actionable previews with **TBD** capabilities.

### Brothel

Music and whispered conversations make this a source of diversion and unofficial information.

- **Buy and sell:** None.
- **Services:** Heal the captain; raise crew morale.
- **Quests:** Treasure maps, wealthy merchant targets, ransom targets, and bounty-hunter leads.

### Pharmacy

The Pharmacy supplies medicine and treats conditions beyond ordinary rest.

- **Buy and sell:** Potions and medicinal herbs as inventory items.
- **Services:** Heal the captain and treat injured or ill crew.
- **Quests:** Deliver potions, herbs, and medical supplies rather than letters.

### Blacksmith

The Blacksmith is the dedicated market for personal combat equipment. See [shops and equipment](shops-and-equipment.md).

- **Buy and sell — current:** Weapons, pistol and cartridges, and armour. Used weapons sell for 50% and armour for 60% of listed price, subject to equipment restrictions.
- **Services — current:** Equip owned melee weapons and armour.
- **Quests:** None planned.

### Fort & Garrison

The Fort provides routine military work and receives prisoners captured at sea.

- **Buy and sell:** None.
- **Services:** Hand over captured pirates or enemies of the controlling nation.
- **Quests:** Hunt wanted captains; resupply ammunition and gunpowder from another colony; patrol for pirates.

### Governor

The Governor offers rare, dangerous, and politically sensitive official commissions.

- **Buy and sell:** None.
- **Services:** None planned.
- **Quests:** High-value bounties, pirate patrols, very important letters, espionage in hostile ports, and capture of ships carrying specified loot.

### Book Store

The Book Store connects collectible books to captain skill progression.

- **Buy and sell:** Skill books.
- **Services:** Read level-gated books to earn skill points. Book names, rarity, level requirements, and rewards remain TBD.
- **Quests:** None planned.

### Bank

The Bank handles finance, valuables, and the recovery of unpaid debts.

- **Buy and sell:** Gold coins, jewelry, precious minerals and metals; rare and extremely expensive weapons.
- **Services:** Deposits and credit.
- **Quests:** Seize money or ships from debtors.

### Weaver

The Weaver is the dedicated clothing market. See [shops and equipment](shops-and-equipment.md).

- **Buy and sell — current:** Clothing, with 15% buyback for eligible unworn pieces.
- **Services — current:** Equip owned clothing.
- **Quests:** None planned.

### Customs House

The Customs House separates cargo regulation and anti-smuggling enforcement from the Harbour Master and Fort.

- **Buy and sell:** No direct trade planned.
- **Services:** Declare cargo and pay duties; clear captured vessels and disputed cargo; resolve confiscations and customs penalties.
- **Quests:** Inspect suspicious ships, conduct anti-smuggling patrols, and recover or deliver confiscated cargo.

## Quest distribution target

| Source | Intended quest scale and identity |
| --- | --- |
| Store | Small and medium freight; rare attacks on commercial competitors |
| Tavern | Rumours, treasure maps, ransom and merchant leads; small escorts |
| Harbour Master | Letters, passengers, large freight, stolen ships; medium and large escorts |
| Shipyard | Ship acquisition, repair-material collection, and vulnerable ship escorts |
| Church | Monks and donation requests |
| Brothel | Illicit rumours, ransom targets, wealthy merchant targets, treasure maps |
| Pharmacy | Medical-item deliveries |
| Fort & Garrison | Routine bounties, resupply, and patrols |
| Governor | High-value bounties, espionage, official letters, and targeted captures |
| Bank | Debt collection and ship seizure |
| Customs House | Inspections, anti-smuggling, and confiscated cargo |

The Store owns small and medium freight. The Harbour Master owns large freight. The Tavern owns small escorts; the Harbour Master owns medium and large escorts.
